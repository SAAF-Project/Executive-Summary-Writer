# Draft Audit Report — Claims Data Management (SYNTHETIC)

> This report is entirely synthetic. The company, figures and findings are invented for testing the
> Executive Summary Writer and do not describe any real organisation or audit.

## Scope and objective

Internal Audit reviewed the design and operating effectiveness of controls over
the quality of claims
data at Example Insurance N.V. for the period 1 January to 30 June. The audit
covered data entry and
validation in the claims administration system, the monthly data quality checks,
the preparation of
management reporting on claims reserves, and the follow-up of previously
reported data issues.

## Overall conclusion

Rating: **Needs improvement**.

A control framework for claims data exists and is documented. Staff in Claims
Operations are
knowledgeable and management was open about known weaknesses during the audit.
However, key controls
are not performed consistently and accountability for claims data quality is
divided across Claims
Operations, Finance and IT.

## Positive observations

- A documented data quality framework with defined controls is in place.
- Automated validation rules at data entry were implemented last year and
  operate as designed.
- Management had identified part of the reporting issue itself before the audit
  started.

## Findings

### F1 — Data quality controls over claims data are not consistently performed (High)

Monthly data quality checks were performed in 3 of the 6 months tested. In the
other months, the
checks were skipped or performed without evidence of review. Exceptions
identified by the checks were
not always resolved: 41 of 118 exceptions sampled were still open after 60 days.
Claims Operations,
Finance and IT each assumed that one of the other departments was responsible
for follow-up.

Management response: Agreed. Claims Operations will perform the monthly checks
and evidence review
from the next quarter. Owner: Head of Claims Operations. Target date: end of Q4.

### F2 — Management reporting on claims reserves contained errors (High)

The quarterly claims reserve report to the Executive Board contained errors in 2
of the last 4
quarters. In both cases the errors originated from unresolved data exceptions in
the source data. One
error overstated the reserve for the motor portfolio; it was corrected in the
following quarter. The
financial effect of the errors has not been quantified by management.

Management response: Agreed. Finance will add a reconciliation between source
data and the reserve
report before each submission. Owner: Head of Financial Reporting. Target date:
end of Q4.

### F3 — Remediation of earlier data issues is not tracked by a single owner (Medium)

Actions from the previous audit of claims data (two years ago) are tracked in
three separate lists
maintained by Claims Operations, Finance and IT. Of 14 agreed actions, 6 are
overdue and 3 could not be
matched to any list. No single function has an overview of progress, and
progress is not reported to
senior management.

Management response: Agreed. Management will appoint a single data owner for
claims data and bring all
open actions under one remediation plan with quarterly progress reporting. Owner
and target date for
the appointment: to be confirmed.

### F4 — User manual for the claims administration system is outdated (Low)

The user manual has not been updated since the last system release. Staff rely
on informal notes.

Management response: Agreed. The manual will be updated. Owner: Claims
Operations. Target date: Q1
next year.
