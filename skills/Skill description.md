### 1. Executive Root Cause Analysis

Identifies and ranks the most likely systemic root causes connecting multiple audit findings. Use this skill when you need to distinguish underlying governance, ownership, capability or control-design weaknesses from individual symptoms before developing an executive narrative.

### 2. Executive Finding Relationship Analysis

Analyses how audit findings relate through possible cause-and-effect links, common drivers and upstream or downstream dependencies. Use this skill when several findings may collectively indicate a broader control weakness or require coordinated remediation.

### 3. Executive Storyline Development

Transforms audit findings, root causes and business impacts into concise Board-level storyline options. Use this skill when you need to explain what is happening, why it is happening, why the Executive Board should care and what management must do.

### 4. Executive Challenge Review

Reviews audit material from the perspectives of an Executive Board member, CRO, regulator and external auditor to identify material information gaps. Use this skill before drafting or finalising an executive summary to test whether business impact, accountability, remediation, regulatory implications and financial exposure are sufficiently clear.

### 5. Executive Board Summary Writing

Produces a concise, three-paragraph Executive Board summary covering the bottom-line message, root cause, business impact, management response and next steps. Use this skill after the analysis and storyline have been agreed and the audit results are ready for Board-level reporting.

### 6. Executive Theme Synthesis

Consolidates multiple related findings into a single executive theme supported by the most relevant evidence and implications. Use this skill when you need to elevate detailed findings into a strategic governance, risk, customer, data, transformation or operational-resilience message for the Executive Board.
